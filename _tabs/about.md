## Personal Philosophy

I don't have life figured out, but I do have a few rules of thumb that have held up so far:

1. **Be honest, then be kind.** Usually both fit in the same sentence.
2. **Ship the imperfect version.** A finished draft teaches more than a perfect plan.
3. **Stay curious.** The moment I think I know everything about something is a good sign I've stopped looking closely.
4. **Rest is part of the work.** I learned this the hard way[^burnout].

> "[A quote that genuinely means something to you, with attribution.]"
